How the mock data is calibrated to Reformation

  • FY2025 net revenue $507.1M; marketing held at ~9% of net revenue for four years (≈ $45M/yr), per the 2026 S-1.
  • AOV $315; DTC ≈ 90% of revenue, e-commerce = 67% of DTC; ~75% of new DTC customers come from outside paid digital.
  • Assumption: ~57% of marketing goes to Google + Meta → ≈ $72K/day combined (Google ~$30K, Meta ~$42K). Older Pathmatics estimates showed Reformation's paid social skews heavily to Instagram.
  • Funnel ranges from apparel benchmarks: Meta CTR ~1.1%, ROAS ~2.5–3x; Google CTR ~1.9%, ROAS ~4–5x (brand search + Shopping lift Google).

Detection logic

  • ROAS = revenue ÷ spend; CAC = spend ÷ conversions (cost per conversion).
  • Baseline = mean ROAS of the 7 days before each day (today excluded so an outlier can't dilute its own baseline). The first 7 days have no baseline and are not scored.
  • Flag when |ROAS − baseline| ÷ baseline > 30%. Severity is Critical at ≥ 50%, else Warning.
  • Explanations compare spend, CTR, CVR, conversions and AOV with their own 7-day baselines and match the pattern to a likely cause.
